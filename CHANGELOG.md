# Changes

## 27 October 2026

### Creatures and breeding

- Hungry Norns periodically bring usable nearby food to mind, helping them eat
  rather than remain distracted by other creatures.
- The optional wolfling food assistance targets hungry Norns without food in sight,
  serving the hungriest first while retaining the food-per-Norn cap.
- Both layouts lift population gates on conception, egg hatching and incubator use.
- Genes needed for reproduction, pregnancy and laying, including reproductive hormone
  half-lives, pass on intact. Other genes continue to mutate normally. This is an
  intentional playability policy, rather than a claim of original genetic behavior.

### Interface and wolfling runs

- Creature medallions display health colour, pregnancy progress and an egg badge;
  tooltips include an approximate time to laying.
- Grendels receive their own names during wolfling runs.
- Runs end and pause when all creatures have died, no eggs remain and no population
  floor can supply a replacement. Active recordings stop with the run.

### Maintenance

- Updated attribution for the integrated LibreCreatures work.
- Made the synthetic lift decision regression independent of random speech and
  installed game artwork.

This update retains the GPLv3 licensing and existing build/packaging instructions.
No game data, personal worlds, internal research or local-only ports are included.
Unattended survival varies with the world and population; complete original-game
compatibility has not been established.
